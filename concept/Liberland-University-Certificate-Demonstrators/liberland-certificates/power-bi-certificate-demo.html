01 / The programme

Build a skill you can use.

Work towards a fictional retail performance dashboard. The focus is reliable calculations and understandable stories, with attention to filters, accessibility and the difference between a report page and a recommendation.

Who this is for

  • Spreadsheet analysts moving into dashboards
  • Managers producing recurring performance reports
  • Learners interested in business intelligence

Before you begin

Required. Confidence with spreadsheet tables, percentages and basic charts. No DAX experience required.

Helpful. Familiarity with relationships between orders, products and customers.

Tools & access

Power BI Desktop on a compatible Windows computer. The browser sample needs no installation; sharing-service permissions are outside the demo.

Study hours include explanation, guided practice, independent activities and assessment. Software setup may require additional time. Actual delivery dates, support and fees are not set in this demonstrator.

02 / Learning outcomes

What you will be able to do.

  1. Prepare data using repeatable transformations
  2. Model related tables at a clear level of detail
  3. Create basic measures and explain filter context
  4. Select visuals that answer a business question
  5. Build an accessible, coherent report journey
  6. Validate totals and present a decision-focused story
03 / Programme structure

6 modules. 24 purposeful hours.

Open a module to explore its topics and practical activity. Hours are the total learning allocation, not video duration.

01Questions & report planning2 hours
Topics

Audience; decisions; KPI definitions; page sketches.

Practical activity

Sketch a report answering where margin is falling.

02Data preparation4 hours
Topics

Power Query steps; types; missing values; merging and appending.

Practical activity

Prepare orders and products while preserving a traceable transformation sequence.

03Modelling & relationships4 hours
Topics

Fact and dimension tables; keys; grain; relationship direction; date table concepts.

Practical activity

Create a small star model and check that joins do not duplicate sales.

04Measures & filter context5 hours
Topics

Measures versus columns; SUM; DIVIDE; CALCULATE introduction; filter effects.

Practical activity

Build revenue, margin and margin-rate measures and reconcile them to source totals.

05Visual storytelling4 hours
Topics

Visual selection; hierarchy; slicers; accessible colour; tooltips; narrative.

Practical activity

Create overview and detail pages that guide a reader from finding to evidence.

06Dashboard project & validation5 hours
Topics

Report testing; annotated findings; delivery and assessment.

Practical activity

Submit a validated report and a short walkthrough of the business decision.

Total: 24 hours · Final project and assessment are included in the final module.

04 / Apply your learning
The final project

Retail performance story: identify which region warrants a margin investigation.

Your submission

  • A report file with a documented model and repeatable preparation steps
  • Measures, an overview page and a supporting detail page
  • A validation checklist and a three-minute walkthrough script

The downloadable CSV is a tiny sample-lesson dataset. Full programme datasets and teaching materials remain to be developed.

Assessment & feedback

Proposed assessment: module practice portfolio 40%; final project 60%. Formative lesson checks allow retries and do not contribute to the award score.

Final-project marking criteria

  • Data model and measure accuracy — 40%
  • Report usability and accessibility — 30%
  • Evidence, validation and narrative — 30%

A tutor would review the submitted work against these criteria. The browser check below assesses only the sample question.

05 / Completion

How the certificate would be earned.

A proposed Liberland University certificate of completion would record the programme title and 24 study hours after the requirements are met. This demo makes no claim of academic credit or external accreditation.

  • Complete all modules and submit the practice portfolio and final project.
  • Achieve at least 60% overall and at least 60% on the final project.
  • Meet the programme’s essential checks listed below and acknowledge any external help.
  • Revise work after feedback if needed; proposed resubmission arrangements require academic approval.

Essential programme check: Report totals must reconcile and relationships must not inflate measures.
